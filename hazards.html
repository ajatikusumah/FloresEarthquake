Fault and historical context

Source parameters associate the mainshock with the Flores Back-Arc Thrust, assessed capable of up to M7.8–M7.9.

  • 1961 North Flores earthquake
  • 1992 Flores earthquake and tsunami — widely cited at roughly 2,500 deaths, one of Indonesia's deadliest modern tsunami disasters
  • 1982 Solor earthquake
  • 2003 Manggarai earthquake
