Compounding hazards and protection risks

SCIDN frames the earthquake as layering on top of pre-existing vulnerabilities rather than an isolated event: an ongoing El Niño-influenced drought and recurring eruption hazard from Mount Lewotobi. Flagged protection risks during the response include unaccompanied children, family separation, institutionalisation/orphanage risk, and heightened child-trafficking risk.
