Situation overview

Executive summary

BMKG, Basarnas/ANTARA and credible media reporting

A shallow M7.7 tectonic earthquake occurred offshore approximately 30 km northeast of Mbay, Nagekeo, producing shaking up to MMI VII. BMKG detected tsunami waves at nine observation points, with a preliminary maximum of 0.94 m at Maurole–Ende; the tsunami warning has ended. BNPB's field-validated update at 08:00 WIB recorded 1 death, 4 injuries, approximately 2,000 people undertaking self-evacuation in Nagekeo, and preliminary damage to homes and public facilities. Aftershocks continue and all impact figures remain subject to further BNPB/BPBD verification.

Confirmed by BNPB

Validated at 08:00 WIB
  • 1 confirmed death in Sikka Regency and 4 injured people in Sikka and Nagekeo.
  • Approximately 2,000 residents in Nagekeo undertook self-evacuation.
  • 1 household (6 people) was reported affected in Bima City.
  • The tsunami warning ended at 07:31:30 WIB; aftershock vigilance remains necessary.

Preliminary damage

Further field verification required
  • 2 houses heavily damaged, 1 moderately damaged and 2 slightly damaged.
  • 1 Bulog warehouse affected.
  • 3 public facilities slightly damaged and 6 government offices affected.
  • Electricity remained disrupted in Nagekeo at the time of the BNPB update.

Not yet available

Critical information gaps

Operational implications

Priorities for the first 24 hours

1

Search, rescue and triage

Prioritize Lorens Say/Maumere Port and collapsed-building sites; activate trauma referral and standardized casualty registration.

2

Building and health-service safety

Conduct rapid safety assessments of hospitals, health centres, schools, hotels, places of worship, ports and bridges before reuse.

3

Displacement and vulnerable groups

Map displacement sites and needs for water, sanitation, medicine, pregnant women, children, older people, persons with disabilities, tourists and chronic patients.

4

Risk communication and aftershocks

Use one official message source; prevent return to cracked buildings and beaches, tsunami misinformation and aftershock-related injury.

Consistent public messaging

Remain calm and use official BMKG, BNPB and BPBD channels. The tsunami warning has ended, but do not enter cracked or damaged buildings. Stay alert for aftershocks, protect your head during shaking, and move to an open area once shaking stops. Follow local government instructions before returning to homes, schools, hotels or public facilities.
