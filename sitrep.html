Situation overview

Executive summary

BMKG, BNPB, BPBD NTT and IHCP sources

A shallow M7.7 tectonic earthquake occurred offshore approximately 30 km northeast of Mbay, Nagekeo, producing shaking up to MMI VII and a locally observed tsunami at 10 monitoring locations. The warning ended at 07:31:30 WIB. BNPB's preliminary update at 15:00 WIB recorded 38 deaths, 2 serious injuries, 11 minor injuries and approximately 2,000 evacuees or self-evacuees. Detailed housing and sectoral damage counts remain based on the earlier 12:30 WIB update while field verification continues. Basarnas operational figures are presented separately because their scope and cut-off differ. Animal-health impacts and official monetary damage-and-loss estimates were not yet available. All figures remain subject to continuing BNPB/BPBD and sector-agency validation.

Operational implications

Priorities for the first 24 hours

1

Search, rescue and triage

Prioritize Lorens Say/Maumere Port and collapsed-building sites; activate trauma referral and standardized casualty registration.

2

Building and health-service safety

Conduct rapid safety assessments of hospitals, health centres, schools, hotels, places of worship, ports and bridges before reuse.

3

Displacement and vulnerable groups

Map displacement sites and needs for water, sanitation, medicine, pregnant women, children, older people, persons with disabilities, tourists and chronic patients.

4

Risk communication and aftershocks

Use one official message source; prevent return to cracked buildings and beaches, tsunami misinformation and aftershock-related injury.

Consistent public messaging

Remain calm and use official BMKG, BNPB and BPBD channels. The tsunami warning has ended, but do not enter cracked or damaged buildings. Stay alert for aftershocks, protect your head during shaking, and move to an open area once shaking stops. Follow local government instructions before returning to homes, schools, hotels or public facilities.
