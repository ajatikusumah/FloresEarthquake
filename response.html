The eight-point directive in full

1. Accelerate emergency-status declarations

Six priority regencies and NTT Province urged to issue status decrees the same day — required before BNPB and other agencies can channel full support.

Legal & administrative
2. Stand up unified posko

Regency/city and provincial posko formed rapidly, commanded by TNI–Polri unit commanders under one integrated command.

Command structure
3. National & tactical posko locations

Main National Assistance Posko in Labuan Bajo; Tactical Posko in Maumere covering Sikka, Nagekeo, Ngada.

Command structure
4. National logistics posko at Halim

Central receiving point at Halim Perdanakusuma for ministries, SOEs, private-sector and public assistance, routed onward by air.

Logistics
5. Basarnas-led SAR

Joint TNI–Polri–volunteer teams under Basarnas' direct command until no trapped or missing victims remain.

Search and rescue
6. 7-day restoration target + parallel data collection

Limited electricity/water/fuel/comms restoration targeted within 7 days; house-damage data collected in parallel for Huntara/repair planning.

Infrastructure
7. Cross-agency air/sea assets

TNI, Basarnas and BNPB helicopters, ships and aircraft focused on reaching isolated areas with food, blankets and tents.

Logistics
8. Daily Media Center briefings

Official briefings daily at 17:00 WITA from 16 August, for transparent day-to-day updates and to reduce misinformation.

Public information
