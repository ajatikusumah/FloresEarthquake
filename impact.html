Latest official impact figures

BNPB's newest official infographic, 18 Aug 2026, 09:00 WIB
  • 70 deaths and 665 injured — up from 56 deaths / 277 injured the previous day.
  • 31,220 people displaced and 16,411 households (KK) affected — the KK figure grew far faster than displacement, possibly reflecting a broader definition rather than a literal 10× jump.
  • 12,153 houses damaged (6,578 heavy / 1,660 moderate / 3,915 light) — up from 4,674 the previous day.
  • Health facilities affected fell sharply to 83 (9 hospitals + 74 puskesmas) from 211 — the only sector figure to drop, now close to Kemenkes's own long-standing count.
  • Emergency-response status confirmed unchanged for a third consecutive report across all 7 priority regencies plus NTT Province.

Preliminary damage and response logistics

BNPB 18 Aug 09:00 WIB infographic (newest)
  • 12,153 houses damaged province-wide (6,578 heavy / 1,660 moderate / 3,915 light) — roughly 2.6× the previous day's total of 4,674.
  • 264 education, 157 worship, 171 government-office, 116 public facilities and 34 road points (unchanged for a third report) affected; 1 market and 5 bridges newly tracked this round.
